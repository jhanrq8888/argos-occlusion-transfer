\noindent\textbf{Registered versus reported (obs. 14).} The programme began with H1, H2 and H5 (\texttt{PROTOCOLO.md}, 11 September 2026, and \texttt{PROTOCOLO\_v2.md}): H1 predicted that YOLOv11s with a CBAM attention module and augmentation (V4) would beat V1 on occluded ExDark without losing more than 10\,\%% FPS, and did not hold (V4$-$V1 $-$3.29 pp on severe occlusion); H2, that augmentation would contribute more than CBAM (V3$-$V1 against V2$-$V1), and did not hold ($-$2.43 against +0.18 pp); H5, that the synthetic gain of silhouettes would be smaller than that of patches, and held. Their 14 runs (variants V2 and V4 and two controls) are among the 133 (run table) and their verdict is in \texttt{informes/veredictos\_H1\_H7.md}. The registered H11 cell was CityPersons visibility 0.20--0.50 ($n=432$): +1.44 [$-$0.64; +3.34] (not confirmed); Table I gives \emph{heavy} for comparison with the other blocks. H13 predicted AUC(patches) $>$ AUC(silhouettes) $>$ AUC(diffusion) with DINOv2: the first difference held (+0.043 [+0.031; +0.057]) and the second did not (+0.015 [$-$0.003; +0.034]), hence ``partially confirmed''.
